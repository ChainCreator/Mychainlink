What Is My Chain Link?

My Chain Link is a social networking platform designed for authentic connections. Unlike traditional social media that prioritizes curated highlight reels, My Chain Link focuses on real-time, camera-captured content that reflects genuine moments.

The platform enables users to:

  • Share authentic content through camera-only posting — no filtered gallery uploads
  • Connect directly with free messaging and video calling
  • Monetize content through flexible subscription models with direct PayPal payouts
  • Maintain control over their audience without algorithmic interference

Platform Features

  • Camera-First Content: All posts are captured in real-time through the device camera, ensuring content authenticity
  • Direct Messaging: Free text and media messaging between connected users
  • Live Streaming: Real-time broadcasting with integrated chat functionality
  • Video Calling: Encrypted peer-to-peer video communication
  • Creator Monetization: Flexible paywall options allowing creators to set their own pricing
  • Music Integration: Spotify sharing to connect through music

How It Works

Users create accounts via email, Google, or Facebook authentication. Once registered, they can:

  • Build a profile with bio, location, and profile photo
  • Post camera-captured content to their feed
  • Discover and connect with other users
  • Exchange messages and video calls
  • Subscribe to premium content from creators

Security & Privacy

My Chain Link implements industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • Row-level security policies on all database tables
  • Two-factor authentication support
  • Complete account deletion with zero data retention

Monetization Model

Creators can monetize their content through subscriber-only posts and profiles. Key features include:

  • Creator-defined pricing for premium content
  • Direct PayPal integration for instant payouts
  • No platform-imposed revenue thresholds
  • Flexible content locking at post or profile level
