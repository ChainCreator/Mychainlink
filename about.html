Why we built this

What My Chain Link is

My Chain Link is a social network for real connections. Most platforms reward the highlight reel: the retouched photo, the third take, the post timed for peak engagement. We went the other way. Everything you post here is captured live through your camera, right when it happens.

What that gives you:

  • Real posts. Camera-only capture — no gallery uploads, no filter stack, no month-old photo passed off as today.
  • Direct contact. Free messaging and video calling between people in your chain.
  • Money that reaches you. Subscription pricing you set yourself, paid out straight to PayPal.
  • Your own audience. A chronological chain, not a feed reordered by a ranking model.

What is inside

  • Camera-first posting — every post is captured in the moment, so what you see actually happened.
  • Messaging — free text and media between connected accounts.
  • Live streaming — broadcast in real time with chat running alongside.
  • Video calls — encrypted peer-to-peer, no third party in the middle.
  • Creator paywalls — lock a single post or a whole profile, at a price you choose.
  • Music — share what you are listening to through Spotify.

How it works

Sign up with an email address, Google, or Facebook. From there:

  1. Build your profile — photo, bio, location, and your birthday for the badge on your day.
  2. Post to your chain straight from the camera.
  3. Find people worth following and link up.
  4. Message them, call them, keep the thread going.
  5. Subscribe to creators whose work you want more of.

Security and privacy

The account protections we run:

  • Encrypted transport on every request (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Authentication through Supabase Auth
  • Row-level security on every database table
  • A 12-character password floor, plus two security questions stored only as hashes
  • Optional two-step PIN on your account
  • Full account deletion with nothing retained afterwards

Your birth year is used to confirm you are 13 or older and is never shown to anyone else. Other people only ever see the day and month.

How creators get paid

Creators earn from subscriber-only posts and subscriber-only profiles:

  • You set the price — there is no house rate
  • PayPal payouts, no waiting on a minimum balance
  • No revenue threshold before you can withdraw
  • Lock content at the post level or the profile level, whichever fits
