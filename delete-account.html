Delete your account

It is your data. You can take all of it off our servers whenever you want, and we will not make you argue with a chatbot first.

This is permanent. Once it is done there is nothing to restore.

What gets removed

  • Your profile and personal details — name, email, birthday, bio, location, avatar
  • Every post, photo, and video you made
  • Your comments, reactions, and messages
  • Your links and follower relationships
  • Your subscription and payout details
  • Your login credentials, security answers, and two-step PIN

How to do it

  1. Sign in to your account at mychainlink.ca
  2. Open Settings from the bottom navigation
  3. Choose Delete Account
  4. Enter your password to confirm

The account disappears from the app immediately. Everything behind it is wiped from our servers within 30 days.

Locked out of your account? Email privacy@mychainlink.ca from the address you signed up with and we will handle it for you.
